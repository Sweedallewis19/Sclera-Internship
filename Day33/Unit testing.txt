I began by learning the fundamentals of unit testing and its importance in the software development lifecycle. 
I explored how unit testing differs from integration and system testing, focusing on testing individual components in isolation.
I gained hands-on experience with JUnit 5, which is widely used for writing and executing test cases in Java-based applications. 
I used various annotations such as @Test, @BeforeEach, and assertions like assertEquals and assertThrows to validate expected outcomes.

To isolate components and avoid dependencies on external systems like databases, I learned to use Mockito for mocking objects. 
By using annotations such as @Mock, @InjectMocks, and @MockBean, I was able to simulate the behavior of repository and service layers. 
This helped in testing business logic independently and efficiently.
